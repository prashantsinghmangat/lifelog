Manifesto

Your life doesn't need another AI.

It doesn't need to analyse you. It doesn't need to optimise you. It doesn't need to become a training set. It just needs somewhere quiet to stay.

No model trains on your life

Your entries belong to you. Receipts, schedules and private thoughts are never fed to a machine-learning dataset.

No streaks, no guilt

Take a week off and nothing breaks. LifeLog never scores your days or manufactures pressure to keep a number alive.

No noise

One capture box holds everything. No nested folders, no tag trees, no schema to maintain before you can write a line.

No ads, no profiling

Your log is not an advertising profile. Nothing is sold, and no sponsored row will ever appear in your day.

Trust & architecture

Your data stays yours.

No claims about local-only magic that isn't there. LifeLog syncs through an authenticated Postgres database with row-level security, isolated to your account, and exportable in open formats whenever you want it back.

Strict account isolation

Row-level security means only your authenticated session can read or write your rows — enforced at the database, not in the app.

One-click open export

JSON and CSV of the whole archive, any time. No proprietary format, no lock-in, nothing you can't take with you.

Biometric app lock

Native fingerprint and face unlock on Android keeps a borrowed phone from becoming an open diary.

No trackers, no ad networks

No third-party analytics SDK watching how you use the app, and no telemetry leaving the device about what you log.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Does LifeLog use AI to read my entries?

No. Entries are parsed by plain, deterministic rules that run on your device, and retrieved with ordinary database queries. Nothing you write is sent to a model or used as training data.

How long does it take to log something?

Under five seconds. You type one natural line — ₹350 lunch, 2h client deck, dentist 4pm — and press enter. There is no project picker, no category tree and no timer to remember to stop.

What happens if I stop logging for a week?

Nothing. LifeLog has no streaks, no scores and no guilt notifications. You pick it back up whenever you want and the record carries on.

Where is my data stored, and can I get it out?

Your entries live in an authenticated Postgres database with row-level security, isolated to your account. You can export the whole archive as JSON or CSV at any time, with no lock-in.

Which platforms does LifeLog run on?

Android, as an installable app, and any modern browser as a web app. Android is the platform the app is built and tested for first.
