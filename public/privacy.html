lifelog — privacy

Last updated 2026-10-01. Describes what the app does today — not what a future store listing or jurisdiction might separately require.

What's stored, and where

Your entries live in a Supabase Postgres database, in one table, protected by a row-level-security policy that is the application-level, database access control restricting each account to its own rows — not a guarantee covering every copy of the data (the nightly backup below intentionally uses a privileged key that bypasses it).

Deleting an entry hides it from the app immediately; the row itself is retained — including in backups — until it ages out automatically. A nightly backup copies the table to Netlify Blobs and keeps the last 30 days of snapshots, which also include anything you've deleted within that window.

Photos are stored only in this device's local browser storage (IndexedDB). They are not uploaded or synchronized to LifLog's servers by the app.

Account data

Signing in (rather than using guest mode) is handled by Supabase Auth, which holds your email address, a password if you chose that sign-in route, magic-link/one-time-code state while you're signing in, and your session/refresh tokens.

What isn't collected

No analytics, telemetry, or tracking library is present in the app. No cookies are used — your session is kept in this browser's local storage only.

Your export

"Export JSON" on the You screen downloads every entry you haven't deleted. It does not include photos.

Reminders

Reminder notifications are scheduled entirely on your device. No push server is involved.

On your Android device

Android's own cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are deliberately excluded for this app. Local storage holds a live session token, and the notification system's own storage holds entry titles — neither is meant to leave the device by that route.

Requesting data deletion

There is no self-service deletion control in the app yet. To request deletion of your data, open an issue at github.com/prashantsinghmangat/lifelog/issues.

What this page doesn't cover

This page describes LifLog as implemented today. It makes no claim about Google Play data safety disclosures, GDPR, CCPA, or any other distribution- or jurisdiction-specific requirement.
